About

Leading digital transformation at IMTA

Ayyan Ali Siddiqui is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of IMTA. As CEO, he leads the company’s overall strategy, operations, and innovation initiatives. With a strong background in technology, digital transformation, and business management, he has successfully positioned IMTA as a full-service digital solutions company providing web and mobile development, AI services, and social media marketing and management.

Under his leadership, IMTA has expanded its services internationally and continues to help businesses transform digitally, improve operational efficiency, and grow their online presence. He is passionate about innovation, technology, and building high-performance teams that deliver impactful results.
